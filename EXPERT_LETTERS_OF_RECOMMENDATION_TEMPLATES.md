---

## Overview for Petition Assembly
USCIS requires that Letters of Recommendation come from **both dependent and independent experts** of distinguished standing. The letters must not simply praise the beneficiary in vague terms; they must specifically describe:
1. The **precise technical breakthrough** achieved by the applicant.
2. Why this breakthrough solved an **open problem in the field** that previous approaches failed to address.
3. The **major significance and widespread adoption** of the applicant's work.
4. Why the applicant's work directly serves the **national interest of the United States**.

Below are four specialized, ready-to-customize recommendation letter templates for target signatories.
